Zencode02 · The logo

02 / The logo

Built from traces

The wordmark is drawn the way a circuit is routed: rounded tracks, each carrying a live coral trace that ends in a node. It reads ZENCODE, and the centre dot stands in for the O.

Zencode03 · Logo anatomy

03 / Logo anatomy

How it's made

1 2 3 4 5
1
Tracks

Heavy cream strokes with fully rounded ends. Corners are hard 90° turns; the C and D use 60° chevrons.

2
Traces

A thin coral line runs down the centre of every track, like a signal through a wire.

3
The dot

The only solid coral shape. It is the O in ZENCODE and the "zen" pause at the centre.

4
Nodes

Every trace ends in a small node. This detail becomes our main graphic device.

5
Descriptor

"Software Labs" in pixel-cut capitals, widely tracked to the full width of the wordmark.

Zencode05 · Clear space & size

05 / Clear space & minimum size

Give it room

xx xx

Clear space = x, where x is the height of one track (the thickness of the Z's top bar). Keep text, edges and other graphics outside this zone.

120 px / 30 mm

Full lockup minimum. Below this, "Software Labs" stops being readable.

80 px / 20 mm

Below 120 px, drop the descriptor and use the wordmark only.

24 px / 6 mm

ZE monogram minimum. For 16 px favicons use the prepared favicon files.

06 / Logo misuse

Please don't

The logo is drawn precisely. Always use the supplied files and never redraw, rearrange or restyle them. If none of the versions fits, ask before improvising.

Zencode08 · Colour pairings

08 / Colour pairings

Make it readable

Contrast ratios against WCAG 2.2. Body text needs 4.5:1. Coral on charcoal and cream on coral pass only for large text (24 px+, or 19 px bold), so keep those for headlines and buttons.

Zencode11 · Graphic elements

11 / Graphic elements

The toolkit

Every device comes from the logo: tracks, traces, nodes and pills. Use them sparingly as structure and signals, never as wallpaper.

Trace & node

A 2 px coral line ending in an 8 px dot. Use it for dividers, pointers, progress and connections.

Code pills

Stacked capsules from the monogram, like lines of code. Good for loaders, placeholders and pattern.

Glyph field

A quiet dot grid that comes alive around the cursor on the website. In print, use it as texture.

Sections are counted, like a well-commented file: "01 / Label", in coral capitals.

Zencode12 · Voice & tone

12 / Voice & tone

Talk like a builder

We sound like the person who'll actually build the thing: clear, direct and warm, with a bit of wit. Short sentences. Real outcomes. No buzzword soup.

01

Plain, not technical

Explain what it does for the business before how it works. If a client wouldn't say the word, we don't either.

02

Honest, not hyped

AI where it's useful, with a human in the loop. We don't over-promise, invent numbers or call illustrations real data.

03

Confident, not corporate

A small studio that's proud of it. Punchy headlines, a little play ("Big talk? Small shop."), and an easy next step.

We say

  • "You think it. We build it."
  • "A better way to get the work done."
  • "Give your 'what if' a first version."
  • "We start with a real task, then work out whether AI can help."

We don't say

  • "Leveraging cutting-edge synergies"
  • "End-to-end AI-powered digital transformation"
  • "World-class, best-in-class solutions"
  • "Packages from RM999!"
